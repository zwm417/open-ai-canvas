## AI Prompt Structure

Use this order:

1. Brand board type
2. Core subject
3. Composition and layout
4. Logo or typography relationships
5. Color and lighting
6. Material and texture
7. Brand constraints
8. Negative constraints

### Model Notes

- Gemini / GPT-Image: give explicit layout, board type, subject relationships, and forbidden elements.
- Midjourney: put strongest nouns and layout cues first; do not rely on long accurate text.
- Any model: verify text accuracy, trademark risk, logo legibility, and whether the concept still looks like one coherent brand.
