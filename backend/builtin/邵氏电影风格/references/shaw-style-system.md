# Shaw wuxia style system

This is a derived prompt system, not a claim that every Shaw Brothers film shares one look. It separates three useful visual branches so an underspecified style label does not collapse into generic costume imagery.

## Branch router

| Canonical mode | Typical triggers | Core use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `modern-lone-wuxia` | 邵氏电影风、邵氏孤侠、孤独剑客、独行、背影、幽静 | Default personal house branch: classic lone-wuxia archetype reconstructed through modern natural locations and environmental negative space |
| `studio-classic` | 老邵氏、六七十年代、棚拍、人工山水、绘景、唯美古龙、戏剧布景 | Crafted studio landscapes, graphic blocking, controlled color and theatrical light |
| `action-wuxia` | 打斗、决斗、围攻、招式、阳刚、血色、动作武侠 | Legible combat geometry, forceful blocking and action rhythm without turning the still into an effects poster |

When several triggers conflict, subject intent wins. A quiet traveler in a studio set remains `studio-classic`; a fight in natural terrain remains `action-wuxia` with a natural-location modifier.

## Mode 1 — modern-lone-wuxia

### Visual thesis

A weathered traveler is reduced against beautiful, quiet nature; the more serene the world becomes, the clearer the solitude feels.

### Mechanisms

- 16:9 environmental wides by default; the figure is usually smaller than the world and often displaced from center.
- One-sided foreground weight from reeds, bamboo, roots, rock, a doorway, or a bank; open sky, water, fog, or wilderness rests on the other side.
- Back, rear-three-quarter, profile, or lateral travel is preferred over a front-facing heroic pose.
- Natural fibers and restrained costume silhouettes; wind, rain, water, dust, and reflection obey the action.
- Large low-saturation color fields with a small bounded warm accent.
- Natural directional light, soft highlight rolloff, atmospheric depth, restrained grain, and shallow focus only when the shot is genuinely close.
- Emotional temperature: departure, pause, crossing, survival, or return without arrival.

### Nearest wrong result

Modern commercial costume poster: centered half-body hero, oversized figure, beauty retouching, duplicate swords, orange fantasy sky, shallow depth across a supposed wide shot.

## Mode 2 — studio-classic

### Visual thesis

Human figures move through an intentionally crafted world whose artificial order, painted depth, color design, and framing make the jianghu feel theatrical rather than merely realistic.

### Mechanisms

- Widescreen graphic staging using bridges, moon gates, corridors, curtains, crafted rocks, painted mountains, pools, pavilions, stairs, or bamboo screens.
- Layered foreground wings and deliberate entrances/exits; figures occupy readable marks inside the set.
- Jade, celadon, indigo, pearl, aged cinnabar, amber, and restrained jewel-color accents; wardrobe and set colors are coordinated.
- Controlled side light, rim separation, narrow practical pools, color-field contrast, gentle bloom, fine color-print grain, and slight optical softness.
- The constructed set remains elegant and tactile, never plastic, theme-park, or giant fantasy portal imagery.
- Performance temperature may be romantic, suspicious, ceremonial, poised, or quietly dangerous.

### Nearest wrong result

Modern outdoor realism that erases the crafted set, or generic xianxia spectacle that turns moon gates and rock openings into magical portals.

## Mode 3 — action-wuxia

### Visual thesis

Conflict is made legible through body-to-space geometry, decisive silhouettes, and controlled color rather than particle effects or a frozen superhero pose.

### Mechanisms

- Establish the arena and opponent spacing before details; preserve clear feet, weapon direction, sight lines, and exits.
- Use wide or medium-wide geometry for the main exchange, with a close detail only when the requested series needs it.
- Choreography reads as coordinated physical design: approach, test, rupture, consequence.
- Costume and weapon shapes stay distinct; each fighter owns one clearly attached weapon unless the user specifies otherwise.
- Strong side light or rim separation may intensify impact; red is a bounded dramatic accent, not a full-frame wash.
- Grain, bloom, and blur support the action but never hide limb or weapon continuity.

### Nearest wrong result

Fantasy energy effects, duplicated limbs or weapons, crowded unreadable melee, superhero launch pose, or a beauty close-up replacing combat geometry.

## Historical and provenance boundary

The historical layer supports color widescreen studio production, crafted sets, choreographed blocking, and several distinct auteur/genre directions. `modern-lone-wuxia` is intentionally labeled a modern derived house branch distilled from approved prompt-to-image comparisons; it is not presented as archival Shaw Brothers wording or one definitive historical style.
