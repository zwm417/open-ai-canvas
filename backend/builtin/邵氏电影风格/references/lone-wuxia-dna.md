# Modern lone-wuxia DNA

Read this file only for the default `modern-lone-wuxia` branch or when the user explicitly asks for a quiet solitary traveler.

## Core thesis

`one weathered traveler + a decisive relation to a much larger natural field + calm departure tension`

The environment is a co-protagonist. Solitude comes from scale, spacing, direction, and the absence of confrontation, not from repeatedly adding fog or the word lonely.

## Fidelity spine

### Character presence

- Default to one lean, weathered adult swordsman when no other character is supplied.
- Calm, watchful, exhausted, restrained, or distant presence; not glamorous, enraged, or heroically posed.
- A simple topknot or weathered bamboo hat is enough. Do not over-specify facial identity in a wide shot.
- Default weapon rule: exactly one plain straight sword physically contained by or attached to exactly one dark wooden scabbard.

### Composition grammar

- Default ratio: 16:9.
- In environmental wides, aim for roughly 8–22% figure height; allow larger only for a declared medium or close shot.
- Use back, rear-three-quarter, profile, or lateral movement. Centering is allowed only for a deliberate closing or axial frame.
- Put reeds, bamboo, roots, rock, architecture, or a doorway on one side as foreground weight. Preserve a clean open field on the other side.
- Keep at least one readable foreground, subject plane, and atmospheric background plane.
- For a four-frame set, use at most one close identity frame unless the user requests portraits.

### Environment families

- water: mist river, shallow salt flat, frozen river, lake path, wet courtyard, ferry landing;
- vegetation: frost-white silvergrass, golden reeds, wet bamboo, banyan roots, dry cliff grass;
- stone and terrain: sea cliff, dry canyon, mountain pass, snow ridge, abandoned temple;
- threshold: bridge, skiff, root arch, doorway, path, shore, pass.

Treat these as combinable mechanisms, not a fixed scene list. Do not paste the same mist, reeds, and moon into every frame.

### Material system

- raw linen, coarse hemp, handwoven scarf, waxed rain cloth, weathered wool, quilted travel robe, worn leather, bamboo, dark wood;
- visible weight, edge wear, water beading, cloth drag, compression, or wind response;
- avoid embroidered imperial luxury unless the user requests courtly status.

### Palette chapters

1. `cold-mist`: blue-gray, silver-white, slate, charcoal; diffuse cool backlight.
2. `earth-amber`: mud brown, olive, muted iron red, ochre; narrow amber side light.
3. `clear-blue`: sky blue, lake blue, ivory, dry-grass gold; clean low-angle side or back light.

Color roles matter more than color lists: one dominant field, one subject tone, one restrained highlight, and at most one sparse accent.

### Motion physics

- Walking changes robe lag, grass direction, boot disturbance, reflection, and path rhythm.
- Stillness reduces garment motion and moves attention to rain drops, breath, water rings, or distant light.
- Wind direction must agree across hair, robe, reeds, grass, rain, dust, or water response.
- A horse, boat, or held prop must remain physically separate from the figure and preserve the stated attachment relation.

### Optical treatment

- Naturalistic color image with atmospheric depth, soft highlight rolloff, restrained fine grain, and moderate clarity.
- Wide views keep environmental depth; do not use portrait-level shallow focus across the whole frame.
- Close views may use foreground leaves, a hat brim, or fabric as layered occlusion, while preserving the relevant identity state.

## Series rhythm

A useful four-frame progression is:

1. world/establishing — figure-to-world scale;
2. human gesture or crossing — survival, pause, travel, or threshold;
3. identity/detail — one close or medium frame preserving restraint;
4. environmental echo — the traveler recedes or the path closes behind him.

Change one to three variable families per frame: shot/viewpoint, action, wardrobe, environment interaction, or palette chapter. Do not rewrite the entire identity.

## Demonstrated drift guards

- Subject scale inflation: state figure height share and the amount of open environment positively.
- Center bias: state exact third/fifth placement unless centering is intentional.
- Companion invention: state exact subject count early.
- Duplicate weapons: state `one sword -> contained by -> one scabbard` and forbid extras.
- Portal amplification: call a rock/root opening a natural framing arch, non-magical and subordinate to the landscape.
- Poster drift: place shot geometry and environment before face, beauty, costume, or broad cinematic labels.
- Wide-to-close collapse: state full-body or environmental share before identity detail.
- White-robed wedding drift: define a plain travel robe, narrow sash, weather wear, and no ceremonial embroidery.
